# 🎬 H.O.P.E. — Cinematic Visual Prompt Bible
## Unified Art Direction / Crisis Cards / Keyframes (with Cinematic Rotoscope DNA)

> **Purpose:** This document unifies every H.O.P.E. crisis-card image under the same visual language: grounded retro-industrial hard sci-fi, practical spacecraft engineering, human-scale desperation, cinematic composition, photorealistic 35mm texture, translated into sophisticated cinematic rotoscope animation.
>
> **Core feeling:** A civilization is trapped inside a machine that is slowly becoming more dangerous than the universe outside it.

---

### 01 — MASTER CINEMATIC STYLE & UNIVERSAL DNA

#### Visual Identity
H.O.P.E. is a generation ship, not a sleek futuristic yacht.
Every frame feels as if it was photographed inside a real, aging industrial spacecraft that has been operating for decades:
- Battered riveted steel, chipped industrial paint, exposed pipes, hydraulic lines and cable bundles.
- Grease, condensation, dust and scratches, yellow/black hazard markings.
- Analog gauges mixed with aging CRTs and practical displays, heavy pressure doors and mechanical locks.
- Utilitarian workwear, believable EVA equipment, practical lighting rather than decorative neon.

#### The Visual Rule: Used future, not shiny future.
**Avoid:** pristine white spaceship interiors, Apple-like minimalism, clean glossy surfaces, superhero armor, fantasy technology, excessive holograms, generic cyberpunk neon, video-game concept-art plasticity, anime, cartoon, cel-shading.

#### Universal Prompt DNA (With Rotoscope Foundation)
When generating any image for H.O.P.E., incorporate this core visual DNA:

```text
Cinematic 16:9 feature-film keyframe, photorealistic live-action foundation translated into sophisticated cinematic rotoscope animation, subtle hand-drawn linework, natural human anatomy and movement, painterly texture, restrained frame-to-frame imperfection, realistic 35mm film grain, grounded adult sci-fi aesthetic, no anime, no cartoon, no cel-shading. Grounded retro-industrial hard sci-fi, used-future generation spaceship H.O.P.E., practical spacecraft engineering, battered riveted steel, exposed pipes and cable trays, worn hazard markings, realistic human proportions, physically motivated industrial lighting, atmospheric haze, floating dust and condensation, deep layered composition, 35mm anamorphic cinema photography, subtle lens flare, shallow cinematic depth of field, photorealistic materials, restrained desaturated color grade, tactile realism. --ar 16:9 --style raw
```

#### Universal Negative Direction
```text
cartoon, anime, glossy CGI, clean futuristic spaceship, Apple-like minimalism, plastic surfaces, fantasy armor, superhero poses, excessive neon, generic cyberpunk city aesthetic, overly saturated colors, toy-like props, perfect pristine clothing, videogame render, artificial 3D look, excessive holograms, impossible architecture, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, extra limbs, duplicated people, floating objects without physical cause, watermark, logo overlay, UI frame.
```
